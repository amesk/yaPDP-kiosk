# Media credits and provenance

## Original kiosk artwork

The following Full HD SVG illustrations were created specifically for
yaPDP-kiosk and are original, stylized artwork rather than photographs:

- `pdp11-system.svg` — stylized PDP-11/70 cabinet and terminal.
- `front-panel.svg` — stylized processor console, indicator lights and switches.
- `teletype.svg` — stylized Teletype Model 33 ASR and paper tape.

No third-party image assets are embedded in these SVG files.
